# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Formula | Business Purpose | Known Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collected Revenue | Sum of Total Price for paid orders | Monitor realized sales | Depends on payment classification |
| Paid Orders | Count of orders classified as paid | Measure completed paid volume | Not a workflow conversion measure |
| Average Ticket | Collected Revenue / Paid Orders | Track revenue per paid job | Sensitive to small samples and service mix |
| Recorded Gross Contribution | Collected Revenue − recorded Part Cost | Directional contribution view | Not net profit; missing labor and overhead; incomplete part-cost coverage |
| Repeat Customer | Customer with 2+ orders | Define returning-customer behavior | Requires an identified customer |
| Repeat Customer Revenue Share | Revenue from repeat customers / identified-customer revenue | Quantify retention importance | Excludes unidentified customers |
| Top-5 Customer Revenue Share | Revenue from top five customers / identified-customer revenue | Assess concentration | Excludes unidentified customers; sensitive to sample size |
| Part Cost Coverage | Orders with explicitly recorded Part Cost / eligible orders | Assess contribution reliability | A recorded zero counts; a blank does not |

Reliability labels: **Strong** ≥90%; **Usable with caution** 70–89.9%; **Limited** 40–69.9%; **Insufficient** <40%.
