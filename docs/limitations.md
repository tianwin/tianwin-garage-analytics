# Limitations

1. Recorded contribution is not net profit.
2. Labor-cost and overhead expenses are incomplete.
3. Historical parts-cost coverage is incomplete.
4. Labor-hour coverage is insufficient for productivity analysis.
5. Service categories are rule-based classifications of free text.
6. Customer metrics exclude unidentified customer records.
7. Dataset size reflects a small growing business and should not be generalized to the broader automotive industry.
8. Observed relationships are descriptive and should not be interpreted as causal.
